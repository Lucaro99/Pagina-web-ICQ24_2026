Contingency and Service Continuity Policy

ICQ24, SRL is committed to ensuring that, in the event of any failure, our clients do not lose any electronic tax receipts and can continue billing.

01
How continuity works

Each client signs their tax receipts within their own system. If our platform or that of the General Directorate of Internal Taxes (DGII) is unavailable, receipts remain securely stored and are automatically transmitted once service is restored.

02
Recovery times
  • Service restoration: maximum [8 hours].
  • Maximum platform data loss: [24 hours]. Receipts also remain stored within each client's system.
03
What we do in each case
  • If the DGII is unavailable: the platform saves the receipts and automatically retries transmission. We inform our clients and apply the DGII contingency procedures.
  • If our platform fails: the team receives an automatic alert and restores the service. Pending receipts are sent as soon as operations resume.
  • If a server is lost: a new one is provisioned and all information is restored from the latest backup.
  • If a backup fails: the team receives an alert and resolves it within the same day.
  • If a client's digital certificate is about to expire: we notify the client in advance so they can renew it.
04
Backup copies

Every day, an encrypted copy of all information and receipts is created at a location separate from the servers. Backups are neither deleted nor overwritten. At least every [six months], we perform a complete recovery test.

05
Client communications

If an outage occurs, we inform affected clients via email and our support portal regarding what is affected and when we expect it to be resolved. Upon completion, we provide a summary of the incident.
