01
Data we collect
  • Website visitors: name, email address, phone number, company, and the message submitted to us.
  • Clients: Tax ID (RNC), corporate legal name, contact information, and representative details. Electronic invoicing clients: data contained within their electronic tax receipts (e-CF), including buyer details: Tax ID or National ID (cédula), name or legal entity name, email address, amounts, and itemized invoice transaction details.
  • Platform users: username, email address, and access logs. Passwords are stored in encrypted format.
02
How we use data
  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Prestar, facturar y dar soporte a nuestros servicios.
  • Issue, transmit, receive, and retain electronic tax receipts for our clients, as mandated by the General Directorate of Internal Taxes (DGII).
  • Comply with statutory legal and tax obligations.
03
Legal basis

We process personal data to fulfill contracts with our clients and statutory obligations under Law No. 32-23 on Electronic Invoicing, its regulations, and the Tax Code. In all other cases, processing is based on the data subject's consent.

04
Electronic tax receipts

Within the electronic invoicing service, ICQ24, SRL processes data on behalf of each issuing client. Each client is the controller responsible for their buyers' data. Receipts are sent to the DGII and to the buyer, and are retained for ten (10) years, as required by law.

05
With whom we share data
  • With the General Directorate of Internal Taxes (DGII), under statutory legal mandate.
  • With buyers who receive each fiscal receipt.
  • With our cloud server and storage infrastructure providers located in the United States and the European Union. Backups are stored encrypted, and these providers do not have access to unencrypted content.
We do not sell, rent, or transfer personal data to third parties for commercial purposes.
06
Security

We protect data with encrypted connections, access restricted by multi-factor authentication, and encrypted backup copies. Full details are available in our Information Security Policy.

07
Your rights

You may request access to, rectification of, cancellation of, or objection to the processing of your personal data. To do so, please write to privacy@icq24.com including a copy of your identification document. We will respond within the statutory timeframes established by Law No. 172-13.

Data that forms part of electronic tax receipts cannot be deleted while statutory retention obligations remain in effect.

This website uses cookies. You may consult our Cookies Policy.
