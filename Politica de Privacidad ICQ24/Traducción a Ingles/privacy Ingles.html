Introduction

This document establishes the guidelines and best practices for cybersecurity management at ICQ24, utilizing the Microsoft Azure cloud and its integrated security components. The primary objective is to guarantee the protection of information assets and business continuity in the face of cyber threats. All employees, contractors, and third parties are expected to comply with the policies and procedures established in this document.

The Information Security Policy (hereinafter, Policy) pursues the adoption of a comprehensive set of measures designed to preserve the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information—which constitute the three fundamental pillars of information security—and aims to establish requirements to protect information, equipment, and technological services that support the majority of ICQ24's business processes.

02
Objective

The objective of this Policy is to define the principles and fundamental rules for information security management. The ultimate goal is to enable ICQ24 to guarantee information security and minimize risks resulting from potential impacts caused by inadequate management.

03
Scope

This document applies to all information generated, processed, stored, and transmitted by ICQ24, regardless of its format or medium, including electronic and physical paper information. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who handle information on behalf of ICQ24. It covers all operating divisions of ICQ24 across the various geographic regions where it maintains a presence.

04
Information Security

1.1. INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION

ICQ24 will implement an information classification system to ensure adequate protection. Classification will be based on criteria of confidentiality, integrity, and availability. The following classification tiers are established:

  • 1. Public Information: That which does not require any special protection and may be disclosed to the general public.
  • 2. Internal Information: That which possesses a degree of sensitivity and must only be accessible by authorized personnel within ICQ24.
  • 3. Confidential Information: That which is highly sensitive and requires special handling and protection to prevent unauthorized disclosure.
  • 4. Restricted Information: That which is of maximum sensitivity and must strictly be accessible only by a designated select group of authorized individuals within ICQ24.

1.2. RESPONSIBILITY

  • Designate an Information Security Officer responsible for overseeing and ensuring compliance with information management policies and procedures at ICQ24 within each specific operational area.
  • All employees, contractors, and third parties must comply with the policies and procedures established in this document and remain responsible for protecting the information to which they have access.

1.3. PROTECTION

  • Establish appropriate access controls to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to confidential or restricted information.
  • Implement technical and physical security measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, theft, or damage.
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICIES
  • Utilize encryption technologies to protect confidential information both in storage and in transit.
  • Establish policies and procedures for the management of portable storage devices, such as USB drives or external hard drives, to prevent the unauthorized disclosure or loss of information.
  • Perform periodic backups of critical information and establish a disaster recovery plan to ensure the availability and recovery of information in the event of incidents.

1.4. APPROPRIATE USE:

  • Establish clear guidelines on the appropriate use of information and communications at ICQ24, including acceptable use policies for corporate email.
  • Prohibit unauthorized or improper use of information, including unauthorized disclosure, intentional alteration or destruction of information, and unauthorized access to systems or databases.
  • Establish strong password policies and periodic password renewal cycles to safeguard access to information.
  • Train all employees, contractors, and third parties on acceptable use policies and procedures, highlighting the importance of safeguarding confidential information and respecting the privacy rights of individuals.
  • Establish policies and procedures for the proper retention and secure disposal of information when no longer needed, including the secure physical destruction of paper records and cryptographic wiping of electronically stored data.

Regular review and updates are recommended to adapt to evolving regulations and organizational needs.

It also establishes the policies and procedures for cybersecurity management at ICQ24 utilizing the Microsoft Azure cloud and its integrated security components.

Implementing these measures will protect information assets, guarantee data confidentiality, integrity, and availability, and strengthen the organization's defensive security posture against cyber threats. Regular updates and reviews of this document are recommended to adapt to changes in the security threat landscape and organizational requirements.

Cybersecurity

This document sets forth the guidelines and best practices for cybersecurity management across ICQ24, utilizing Microsoft Azure cloud services and enterprise security modules. The primary objective is to ensure the protection of information assets and business continuity in the face of cyber threats. All employees, contractors, and third parties are expected to comply with the policies and procedures established in this data security framework.

2.1. SCOPE

This document applies to all ICQ24 systems and services hosted in the Microsoft Azure cloud. It covers aspects related to infrastructure security, data protection, access and authentication, monitoring and threat detection, and legal and regulatory compliance.

2.2. SECURITY POLICY

a. Information Classification and Handling:

  • Define an information classification policy based on confidentiality, integrity, and availability tiers.
  • Establish procedures for secure information handling, including encrypted storage, transmission, and secure disposal.

b. Access and Authentication:

  • Implement a strong password policy enforcing complexity, length, and expiration cycles.
  • Establish role-based access control procedures for managing user accounts and access privileges.
  • Deploy multi-factor authentication (MFA) to enhance access security.
  • Provision bastion virtual machines as isolated jumpbox entry points rather than allowing direct public access to servers.

c. System Protection.

  • Maintain operating systems, applications, and cloud services up to date with the latest security patches.
  • Deploy continuously updated antivirus and antimalware solutions across all systems and services.
  • Establish security policies for mobile devices and remote access, including full-disk encryption and authenticated VPN access.

d. Data Backup and Recovery

  • Perform periodic backups of all data stored in the Azure cloud, ensuring data integrity and availability.
  • Establish disaster recovery and business continuity plans, supported by regular testing and updates.

e. Security Components in Azure.

Multiple integrated services run within the Azure environment, such as Azure Security Center (Microsoft Defender for Cloud), Azure Firewall, Azure Information Protection, Microsoft Sentinel, Azure Backup, Microsoft Entra ID Protection, and Defender for Identity, among other enterprise network security components.

2.3. RESPONSIBILITY AND PROCEDURES
  • f. Designate an Information Security Officer at ICQ24 to oversee and manage the implementation of security policies and measures in Azure.
  • g. Establish clear procedures for security incident management, including detection, notification, investigation, mitigation, and documentation of incidents.
  • h. Conduct regular audits and security reviews of systems and services in Azure to ensure policy compliance and identify potential vulnerabilities or security gaps.
  • i. Promote cybersecurity awareness and continuous training among ICQ24 personnel, including security best practices, phishing detection, and credential management.
2.4. EVALUATION AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
  • j. Perform periodic assessments of cloud security posture in Azure, reviewing and updating security measures in response to new threats and emerging technologies.
  • k. Participate in cloud security certification and compliance programs, such as Microsoft Azure compliance frameworks.
  • l. Establish a feedback channel allowing ICQ24 employees to report potential security anomalies or improvement suggestions.
  • m. Document and maintain this Azure Cybersecurity Management framework up to date, ensuring it remains accessible to all personnel and stakeholders.

Privacy and Personal Data Processing Policy
ICQ24 PERSONAL DATA PRIVACY AND PROCESSING POLICY

A. Introduction

The Political Constitution of Colombia establishes the right of all individuals to know, update, and rectify information collected about them in databases or files of public and private entities. Likewise, it orders those who possess third-party Personal Data to respect constitutional rights and guarantees when collecting, processing, and circulating such information.

ICQ24 (hereinafter "The Company" or "Data Controller"), acting as Data Controller pursuant to Law 1581 of 2012, is committed to regulatory compliance in personal data protection and respecting the rights of Data Subjects. Consequently, it adopts this Personal Data Privacy and Processing Policy (hereinafter "The Policy"), of mandatory application across all activities involving Personal Data Processing and binding upon the Company, its directors, employees, and third parties with whom it maintains contractual or commercial relationships. The use of our services or purchase of our products (hereinafter "Services and/or Products") shall be governed by this Policy.

B. Objective

The objective of this Policy is to guarantee the constitutional and statutory rights of Data Subjects, identify the legitimate purposes for which Personal Data will be processed, and define guidelines for handling inquiries and claims from Data Subjects.

C. Scope

This Policy is of mandatory and strict compliance for the Company, its directors, administrators, employees, sales agents, marketers, allies, and all other third parties who represent it or act on its behalf, or with whom the Company maintains legal, commercial, or conventional relationships.

D. Definitions

The terms defined herein will be used throughout this document and shall have the meanings established below:

  • Privacy Notice: a verbal or written communication option provided by law or the Company to inform data subjects of the existence and means of accessing information processing policies, as well as the purpose of data collection and use.
  • Authorization: prior, express, and informed consent of the Data Subject to carry out Processing. This may be i) written; ii) verbal; or iii) unequivocal conduct reasonably indicating that the Data Subject consented to data processing.
  • Database: an organized collection of Personal Data subject to Processing, whether electronic or physical, regardless of the manner of its creation, storage, organization, and access.
  • Inquiry (Consulta): a request by the Data Subject, authorized representatives, or persons authorized by law, to access the personal information held regarding them in Company Databases.
  • Personal Data: any information linked to or that can be associated with one or more specific or determinable natural persons.
  • Private Personal Data: data that, due to its intimate or confidential nature, is relevant strictly to the Data Subject.
  • Public Personal Data: data classified as such by statutory law or the Constitution, and all data not considered semi-private, private, or sensitive. Examples include data contained in public records, public registries, official gazettes, and final judicial rulings not subject to reserve, data regarding marital status, profession or trade, and status as merchant or public servant. Personal data in the commercial registry of Chambers of Commerce is public.
  • Semi-Private Personal Data: data that does not have an intimate, reserved, or public nature, whose disclosure may interest not only the Data Subject but also a specific sector, group of persons, or society at large (e.g., fulfillment or default of financial obligations, social security affiliation data).
  • Sensitive Personal Data: data affecting the privacy of the individual or whose misuse could generate discrimination. Examples include data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political views, religious or philosophical convictions, trade union membership, human rights or political party affiliation, data concerning health, sex life, and biometric data (fingerprints, iris scans, etc.).
  • Data Processor (Encargado): a natural or legal person that processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.
  • Claim (Reclamo): a request submitted by the Data Subject or authorized persons to correct, update, or delete Personal Data, or when alleging a breach of data protection regulations.
  • Data Controller (Responsable): a natural or legal person, public or private, that alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the database and/or data Processing.
  • Procedural Requirement: a prerequisite that the Data Subject must exhaust before lodging a complaint with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, consisting of a direct claim to the Controller or Processor.
  • Data Subject (Titular): the natural person whose personal data is subject to Processing.
  • Processing (Tratamiento): any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, such as collection, storage, use, circulation, transfer, transmission, update, or deletion. Processing may be domestic (within Colombia) or international (outside Colombia).
  • Transmission (Transmisión): processing of personal data involving its communication within or outside Colombia when intended for processing by a Processor on behalf of the Controller.
  • Transfer (Transferencia): processing that takes place when the Controller and/or Processor sends Personal Data to a recipient who is also a Controller, located within or outside the country.

E. Principles for Personal Data Processing

In the development, interpretation, and application of this Policy, the following principles shall apply:

  • Legality: personal data processing is a regulated activity that must comply with statutory law and related legal provisions.
  • Purpose: personal data processing must serve a legitimate purpose in accordance with the Constitution and the law, which must be clearly communicated to the Data Subject.
  • Liberty (Consent): personal data processing may only be carried out with the prior, express, and informed consent of the Data Subject. Personal data may not be obtained or disclosed without prior authorization, or in the absence of a legal or judicial mandate relieving the requirement of consent.
  • Veracity or Quality: personal information subject to processing must be true, complete, accurate, up-to-date, verifiable, and understandable. The processing of partial, incomplete, fragmented, or misleading data is prohibited.
  • Transparency: the right of the Data Subject to obtain from the Controller or Processor, at any time and without restriction, information concerning the existence of data pertaining to them must be guaranteed.
  • Restricted Access and Circulation: processing of personal data is subject to the limits derived from its nature, statutory provisions, and the Constitution. Processing may only be conducted by persons authorized by the Data Subject and/or by law. Personal data, except public information, may not be made available on the Internet or mass media unless access is technically controllable to restrict knowledge only to Data Subjects or authorized third parties.
  • Security: personal information subject to processing must be handled with the technical, human, and administrative safeguards necessary to ensure record security, preventing unauthorized alteration, loss, consultation, use, or fraudulent access.
  • Confidentiality: all persons who intervene in the processing of non-public personal data are obligated to guarantee the confidentiality of the information, even after the termination of their relationship with processing duties, and may only supply or communicate data when authorized by law.

F. Processing to Which Personal Data Will Be Subject

The Company will process Personal Data in accordance with conditions established by the Data Subject, applicable law, or public authorities to fulfill activities related to its corporate purpose, such as contracting, executing Services, or delivering Products offered.

Personal Data Processing may be conducted through physical, automated, or digital means according to the type and form of information collected.

I. Temporal Limitations on Data Processing

The Company may only collect, store, use, or circulate Personal Data for the duration that is reasonable and necessary according to the purposes justifying Processing, in compliance with applicable statutory, administrative, accounting, fiscal, legal, and historical requirements. Once processing purposes are fulfilled, and absent statutory rules to the contrary, the Company shall proceed with deleting personal data in its possession. Notwithstanding the foregoing, personal data shall be retained when required to fulfill a legal or contractual obligation.

J. Rights of Data Subjects

Data Subjects have the right to:

  • Access, update, and rectify their Personal Data before Controllers or Processors. This right may be exercised, among others, regarding partial, inaccurate, incomplete, fragmented, or misleading data, or data whose processing is expressly prohibited or unauthorized. Proper prior identification of the individual is required to prevent unauthorized access to Data Subject records.
  • Request proof of the authorization granted to the Company, except in statutory cases where authorization is not required pursuant to Article 10 of Law 1581 of 2012.
  • Be informed by the Company, upon request, regarding the use made of their Personal Data.
  • Lodge complaints before the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce for violations of statutory personal data protection provisions.
  • Revoke authorization and/or request data deletion when Processing fails to respect constitutional and statutory principles, rights, and guarantees.
  • Requests for data erasure or revocation of authorization shall not apply when the Data Subject has a legal or contractual duty to remain in the Controller or Processor database.

K. Authorization

1. Authorization for Data Processing and Method of Acquisition.
Those bound by this policy must obtain prior, express, and informed authorization from the Data Subject to collect and process their Personal Data. This requirement does not apply to data of a public nature.
To obtain authorization, the Data Subject must be clearly and expressly informed of the following:

  • The Processing to which their Personal Data will be subject and the purpose thereof;
  • The voluntary nature of replying to questions regarding sensitive data or data concerning children and adolescents;
  • The rights granted to Data Subjects pursuant to Article 8 of Law 1581 of 2012;
  • The legal identification and physical or electronic address of the Company.

Authorization from the Data Subject must be obtained through any verifiable mechanism capable of subsequent consultation, such as the website, forms, printed formats, in-person or social media campaigns, PQR channels, data messages, apps, email, telephone, or platform registration and authentication.
Authorization may also be obtained from unequivocal conduct of the Data Subject reasonably demonstrating that consent was granted. Such conduct must be unambiguous, leaving no room for doubt regarding the intention to authorize Processing.

1. Representations of Data Subjects.
Data Subjects acknowledge and represent that:

  • They were informed regarding the purposes for which collected sensitive data will be used, as described in Section H of this Policy.
  • They understand that sensitive data includes information affecting individual privacy or whose misuse could cause discrimination, such as racial/ethnic origin, political views, religious/philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, health, sex life, and biometric data.
  • They understand the security safeguards implemented by the Company to protect personal data collected and hereby accept them.

1. Authorization for Processing of Sensitive Data.
When collecting sensitive data, the following requirements must be met:

  • Authorization must be explicit;
  • The Data Subject must be informed that they are under no obligation to authorize the processing of such information;
  • The Data Subject must be explicitly informed prior to processing which data elements are sensitive and the purpose of processing;
  • Authorization from a legal representative or guardian is required for processing data of children and adolescents.

1. Authorization for Processing Data of Children and Adolescents
When collecting and processing data of children and adolescents, the following requirements must be met:

  • Authorization must be granted by persons legally authorized to represent the child or adolescent. The legal guardian must ensure the minor's right to be heard and evaluate their opinion taking into account their maturity and understanding;
  • It must be stated that answering questions regarding minor personal data is optional;
  • The Company must ensure that data processing respects children's rights, and commercial activities must have prior, express, and informed parental or guardian consent.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company will only process minor data for the exclusive purpose of delivering Services or Products, or where strictly necessary to execute the Company's corporate purpose.

L. Storage of Personal Data

2. The Data Subject expressly authorizes the Company to store data in the manner deemed most appropriate, provided it meets the required security standards for data protection.

M. Domestic and International Transfers of Personal Data

The Company may transfer data to other Controllers when authorized by the Data Subject, by law, or by judicial/administrative order. For international transfers, the Company ensures that the receiving country provides adequate data protection standards.

N. Domestic and International Transmissions of Personal Data

O. Procedure for Data Subjects to Exercise Their Rights

To exercise Habeas Data rights, Data Subjects or legally authorized persons may submit inquiries, petitions, and claims to the email indicated below in the "Contact" section.

All requests from authorized persons to access Personal Data held by the Company will be channeled through the designated email address, recording the receipt date and requester identity.

Claims must be addressed to ICQ24 and contain at minimum the following information:

  • Name and identification of the Data Subject or authorized representative.
  • Precise and complete description of facts giving rise to the claim.
  • Specific petitions or requested remedies.
  • Physical or electronic address for notifications and status updates.
  • Supporting documentation and relevant evidence to substantiate the claim.

Upon receipt, petitioner identity and legal standing will be verified. Inquiries will be answered within a maximum of ten (10) business days from receipt. If it is not possible to respond within that timeframe, the applicant will be informed of reasons for delay and the new response date, which shall not exceed five (5) additional business days.

If the request or claim is incomplete, the applicant will be required within five (5) days to remedy defects. If two (2) months elapse from the requirement date without submitting requested information, the claim will be deemed abandoned.

The department responsible for handling data subject petitions, inquiries, and claims to access, update, rectify, or delete data and revoke authorization is Customer Service. Requests may be sent to privacy@icq24.com and mvanegas@icq24.com.
